Preroll r017

The biggest surprise in the dataset. Nearly half of all pre-rolls are infused with concentrate — a 2-3× price premium. The infused/non-infused distinction is the most impactful subtype split in the entire catalogue.

156 — 54%

Token signal is strong: "Infused", "Kief Coated", "Diamond Infused", "Live Resin Infused", "Hash Infused", "Hash Hole" all map to infused. Unclassified rate is near zero.

Concentrate r019

Small category (19 listings) but rich in subtype signal. Extraction method is the primary differentiator — rosin (solventless) vs resin (solvent-extracted) are genuinely different products with different price points and consumer bases.

Texture variants (badder, sugar, wax, crumble) within rosin/resin are secondary — they don't change the extraction method or price tier meaningfully enough to warrant their own subtype.

Remaining Categories r020, r021

Low volume and low classifier value — these can be handled with simple rules or skipped entirely.

Tinctures (29)

Mostly oral-drop / oil format — single type. Contains ~5-6 miscategorized topicals from source data (Reunion roll-on, Vlasic Labs bath bombs, Cause Medic scrub). Also one hemp flower (Barbari) and one hemp patch.

Topicals (9)

All balm format. Ayrloom, Papa & Barkley, Doctor Solomons, Hepworth. No subtype split needed at this volume.

Merch (149)

Accessories, apparel, lighters, grinders, stash jars, rolling papers. Also contains vape batteries (PAX Era Go, CCELL, Vessel, PuffCo) — excluded from strain-matching pipelines regardless of subtype.

Other (9)

Garbage bin: test entries (DELETE, NO ITEM, OLD), 1 miscategorized concentrate (Hudson Cannabis Hash), BOB brand apparel. Cleanup target, not a classifier target.
