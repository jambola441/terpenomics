Research r006 – r011

Vape Product
Taxonomy

What subtypes exist on dispensary menus, which matter for product matching, and what the live data looks like.

The sprint question is whether type should differentiate products in the identity tuple. This research answers what "type" should mean for vape products specifically — the category with the highest internal diversity.

Three Subtypes Worth Keeping r006

All dispensary vape products fall into three functionally distinct groups. Weedmaps and Leafly both surface cartridge and disposable as the two primary consumer-facing subcategories.

🔋

Cart / 510

Pre-filled oil tank, screws onto any 510-thread battery. Universal compatibility. Reuse your battery, replace the cart.


cart
🚀

All-in-One / Disposable

Integrated battery + oil. Self-contained. Discard the whole unit when empty. No accessories needed.


all-in-one
🔌

Pod System

Brand-specific cartridge snaps into a proprietary rechargeable battery. PAX, STIIIZY. Not cross-compatible.


pod

Separately: the ProductCategory enum in models.py already has both vaporizers and cart as distinct values — but the pipeline has never populated cart. It's a latent design intent sitting unused since the model was written.

class ProductCategory(str, Enum):
    flower     = "flower"
    vaporizers = "vaporizers"   # ← everything collapses here
    cart       = "cart"         # ← exists but never populated
    ...

Recommendation r011

Replace the single vaporizers bucket with three typed subtypes. The cart enum value is already there — finish the job.

# models.py — ProductCategory
    flower      = "flower"
    cart        = "cart"        # 510-thread cartridge
    all_in_one  = "all-in-one"  # disposable / AIO
    pod         = "pod"         # brand-specific pod
    # vaporizers ← remove
